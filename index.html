The Story Behind the Lens

b.w fotos was born from more than a love for photography. A name that carries personal history, identity, and meaning.

“b.w” comes from Bradley Wayne, a name spoken often growing up, especially in moments that shaped character and perspective. Over time, that name became identity. That identity became vision.

Photography became a way of seeing the world differently. Black and white imagery removes distraction and reveals what truly matters. Emotion. Light. Story. Connection.

Every photo tells a story. Every story deserves to be remembered.
