The Story Behind the Lens

IDENTITY. STORY. PERSPECTIVE.

b.w fotos was born from more than a love for photography. A name that carries personal history, identity, and meaning.

“b.w” comes from Bradley Wayne, a name spoken often growing up, especially in moments that shaped character and perspective. Over time, that name became more than correction or discipline. That name became identity. That identity became vision.

Photography started as a creative outlet and grew into a way of seeing the world differently. Black and white imagery strips away distraction and reveals what truly matters. Emotion. Light. Story. Connection.

b.w fotos exists to capture real moments. No filters on life. No forced perfection. Just honest images that hold meaning long after the moment has passed.

Every photo tells a story. Every story deserves to be remembered.
